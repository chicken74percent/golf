Quick start

How to play

  1. 1

    Pick a course. All twelve levels are open from the start.

  2. 2

    Pull back. Press on the ball and drag away from your target. The white arrow previews the shot.

  3. 3

    Release to putt. The power bar fills with the length of your pull — 60 pixels is a full swing.

  4. 4

    Find the hole. Sink the ball before the timer reaches zero. Fewer strokes is better.

i

Click the ball, not the green. It sits still only when the last shot has fully stopped.

Field notes

Reading the course

  • White lines are walls. Your ball rebounds off them.

  • Khaki pads reflect the ball and add a 1.5× speed boost.

  • Green is in play. Leave the fairway and the ball — and your stroke count — reset.
